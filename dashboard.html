Empirical Bayes Shrinkage Formulation

To prevent small-sample distortions (such as bilateral associate fixtures dominating IPL superstars), we shrink raw rates toward the population mean:

Shrunk_HP_Strike_Rate = (HP_Runs + Prior_SR * Prior_Weight) / (HP_Balls + Prior_Weight) * Prior_SR = 155.0, Prior_Weight = 50.0 balls Shrunk_HP_Economy = (HP_Runs_Conceded + Prior_Econ * Prior_Overs) / (HP_Balls + Prior_Weight) * 6.0 * Prior_Econ = 9.0, Prior_Weight = 60.0 balls (10 overs)
